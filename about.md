**Yin-Chen (Andy) Chang** — fund manager at E.SUN Asset Management, Taipei.

From 2021 to 2026 I managed two public AI-themed equity funds at First Securities Investment Trust, with combined assets of about NT$8 billion. Before that I spent a decade in international technology research, following Tesla from 2013 and covering AI, robotics, cloud and security software.

My method is first-principles and bottom-up: start from physical capacity and observable prices, derive forward, and only then compare with what companies and the market say. I build the models myself, with AI agents doing most of the engineering, and publish the industry-level layers openly.
